Cookies

This page lists the cookies sessionwithjade.com sets, what each one is for, and which ones I only use if you say yes. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Only if you say yes

If you press Accept all in the cookie banner, or tick them under Choose, the site loads Google Analytics and the advertising tags of Google, Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok. Until you choose, none of them load and nothing is sent.

Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_*, up to 2 years) counts visits and which pages are read. Google Ads (_gcl_au, up to 90 days), Meta (_fbp, up to 90 days), LinkedIn (li_fat_id and LinkedIn's own cookies, up to 180 days) and TikTok (_ttp, up to 13 months) tell me when someone who saw one of my adverts sends an enquiry, joins the mailing list or books a call, so I only keep paying for adverts that work.

They receive the page you visited and the name of that step. They never receive your name, your email address or anything you type into a form. Each of them is a separate controller of what it collects, under its own privacy policy, and may process it in the United States.

Always on

One cookie, session_consent, remembers your choice for six months so you are not asked on every page. It is strictly necessary and holds nothing about you.

Google Fonts serves the typefaces. It sets no cookie, but your IP address reaches Google as part of the request. Calendly sets its own cookies only when you open the booking calendar.

Changing your mind

Use Cookie settings, here or at the foot of any page. Saying no stops the analytics and advertising tags straight away. You can also clear cookies in your browser at any time.
