What does a public speaking coach do?

A public speaking coach helps you prepare and deliver talks with more confidence and impact. With Jade that covers your message, voice, body language, rehearsal and nerves, in one to one sessions in London or online.

Can you help with fear of public speaking?

Yes. Much of Jade’s work is with people who feel sick before they speak, go blank or hear their voice shake. Sessions use practical techniques from performance training, hypnotherapy and NLP so you can stay clear and steady. This is coaching, not medical treatment.

How many sessions will I need?

Most people work in a block of six to ten sessions. If you have a talk coming up soon, share the date in your enquiry and the sessions will be planned around it.

Do you coach online?

Yes. Jade coaches in person in London and online with clients internationally, including people who present in English as a second language.

How much does public speaking coaching cost?

Fees are on application and depend on the number of sessions and any deadline. Make a private enquiry and Jade will reply personally.
