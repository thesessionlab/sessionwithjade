Media & performance preparation

At ease in the moments that matter.

An interview, a press day, a television appearance or a room full of people waiting to hear what you have to say.

Being prepared means more than knowing your material. It means feeling comfortable, speaking clearly and being able to think on your feet when the question is not the one you expected.

I bring twenty years of professional acting into the preparation, so we work on what you say and how you come across, and on the nerves that can get in the way of both.

Preparation can include

  • Television and radio interviews
  • Podcasts and broadcast
  • Press campaigns and junkets
  • Speeches and presentations
  • Panels and live events
  • Camera confidence
  • Launches and announcements
  • Difficult questions

The process

Prepared on the outside and the inside.

01

The moment

We look at the audience, the context, the message and what is at stake.

02

The performance

Language, delivery, voice, presence and how you respond to questions.

03

Rehearsal

Realistic run throughs on camera, reviewed and refined until it feels comfortable.

04

Your state

We work on the nerves and patterns that can interfere on the day.
