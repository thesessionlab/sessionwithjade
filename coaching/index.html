The challenge

You know your subject. Then the moment comes and it doesn’t come out the way you meant.

The board pushes backSomeone questions your numbers and your mind goes blank on an answer you know.
The investor asks something you didn’t expectYou start giving too much detail and lose the point you were making.
You’re put on the spot in a meetingYou rush, over explain, and think of the right reply on the way home.
The presentation that mattersYou prepared for weeks, then everyone looked at you and you sped up.
The conversation you keep putting offYou’ve had it in your head twenty times and you’re still dreading it.
A bigger roleYou sound less sure than you are, and people notice before you say a word.

My approach

The Performance State Method

We work on the person as well as the technique, because how you feel affects how you speak, and how you speak affects how people respond to you.

MindsetWhat goes on in your head before and during the moment: the doubt, the overthinking, the replaying afterwards.
CommunicationWhat you say and how you say it: your opening, your pauses, your answers to the questions you dread.
PerformanceHow you come across on the day: your voice, your body, staying steady when the pressure goes up.

Sessions draw on twenty years of professional performance training, NLP, clinical hypnotherapy, breathwork and communication psychology, chosen for what you need.

What we work towards

What clients come to me to change

Communicate clearly when you’re challenged.
Present your ideas with more conviction.
Stay composed in high pressure conversations.
Speak naturally rather than leaning on an over rehearsed script.
Build a stronger sense of presence in the room.
Handle unexpected questions without losing your thread.
Explain your expertise in a way people understand and remember.

These are things we work towards together. Every client is different and I can’t promise a particular result.
