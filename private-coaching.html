Private coaching

A private space to work on yourself.

Bespoke one to one coaching for people managing demanding lives and careers, when the outside looks successful and the inside feels very different.

You might be carrying a demanding career, going through a significant change, struggling with confidence or finding that old patterns keep coming back however much you understand them.

Our work gives you room to understand what is happening, change what is no longer useful and build a steadier, more confident relationship with yourself. Depending on what you need, sessions combine coaching, clinical hypnotherapy, NLP, breathwork and practical performance techniques.

This is coaching, not medical treatment. If you are working with a diagnosed condition, I am happy to work alongside your GP or therapist.

Areas we work on

  • Confidence and self belief
  • Stress, pressure and burnout
  • Anxiety and overthinking
  • Identity and personal change
  • Relationships and boundaries
  • Sleep and relaxation
  • Professional confidence
  • Patterns and limiting beliefs
