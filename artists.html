Artists & public figures

The pressure of being seen.

Coaching for actors, musicians, artists and people in the public eye, on the personal side of a very public career.

Working in the public eye brings visibility, scrutiny, expectation and creative pressure, usually while you are constantly on the move, and it can be hard to keep hold of yourself in the middle of it.

I have spent twenty years working as an actress, so I know these rooms from the inside. We work on what is happening for you personally and on how you show up, with the same discretion you would expect from anyone on your team.

Areas of work

  • Confidence and self doubt
  • Press and public appearances
  • Performance anxiety
  • Creative blocks
  • Burnout and recovery
  • Public scrutiny and criticism
  • Identity beyond the career
  • Touring and time away
