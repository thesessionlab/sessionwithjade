The approach

We work on the inside and the outside together. Every client is different, so there is no set formula: sessions draw on coaching, clinical hypnotherapy, NLP, breathwork and practical performance training, chosen for what you need and what you want to change.

The Performance State Method brings this together across four areas, perform, create, confidence and regulate, with rehearsal of the real moment built into the work.

Questions

Who is Jade Matthew?

Jade Matthew is a confidence coach and public speaking coach based in London, and a professional actress of twenty years with work for the BBC, HBO, Netflix, Channel 4, ITV and Sky. She is Senior Audio Director at LAMDA, a clinical hypnotherapist and a Master NLP Practitioner, holds a BBC Audio Drama Award and created the Performance State Method.

What does a confidence coach do?

A confidence coach helps you feel and act more sure of yourself in the moments that matter to you, such as meetings, interviews, performances or difficult conversations. With Jade that means working on what is going on inside you and on how you come across, then rehearsing the real situation until it feels natural.

Do you offer public speaking coaching?

Yes. Jade coaches presentations, pitches, keynotes, panels and interviews in London and online, covering your message, voice, body language, rehearsal and nerves. See public speaking coaching.

Who do you work with?

Artists, musicians, actors, public figures, founders, executives and leaders, along with their agents, managers and publicists, and anyone who wants to feel more confident when it counts.

Where are sessions held and how much do they cost?

Sessions are in person in London or online, for clients in the UK and internationally. Coaching is usually a block of six to ten sessions, with fees on application. Start with a private enquiry.
