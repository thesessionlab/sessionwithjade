The approach

We work on the inside and the outside together. Every client is different, so there is no set formula: sessions draw on coaching, clinical hypnotherapy, NLP, breathwork and practical performance training, chosen for what you need and what you want to change.

The Performance State Method brings this together across four areas, perform, create, confidence and regulate, with rehearsal of the real moment built into the work.

About Jade Matthew

With twenty years as a professional actress across television, film, radio and video games, including work for the BBC, HBO, Netflix, Channel 4, ITV and Sky and a BBC Audio Drama Award, Jade knows what it is to perform when people are watching.

She is Senior Audio Director at LAMDA, a clinical hypnotherapist and a Master NLP Practitioner, and has coached more than 500 people, from public figures and performers to founders, leaders and people going through a period of change.
