The Performance State Method

My method bridges the gap between internal and external.

A therapist usually works on what's going on inside you. A voice coach or media trainer works on what people see. I do both, because your nerves change how you sound, and how you stand changes how you feel. It's called the Performance State Method, and everything on this site uses it.
