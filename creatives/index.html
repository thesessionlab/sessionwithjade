Neuro sessions

Breathe in Breathe out

Longer out breath

In for 4, out for 6. For when your heart is racing.

In Hold Out Hold

Box breathing

In, hold, out, hold, 4 seconds each. Good just before you walk on.

In, then in again Long sigh out

Double breath in

Two breaths in through the nose, one long sigh out. For when it all feels like too much.

If you feel dizzy, stop and breathe normally.
