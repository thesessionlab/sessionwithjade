How I work

The Performance State Method.

We work on the inside first: the worries, the patterns and how your body reacts. Then on the outside: what you do, say and choose each day. You leave every session with something you can use that week.

  1. 01Clinical hypnotherapy to calm the reactions that kick in before you can think
  2. 02NLP to change how you talk to yourself
  3. 03Neuro sessions breathwork to settle your body in a couple of minutes
  4. 04Coaching small, practical changes you actually stick to
  5. 05The Session app hypnosis recordings and support between sessions

Is this therapy?

It's coaching that uses clinical hypnotherapy, NLP and breathwork. It isn't medical treatment. If you have a diagnosed condition, or you're already seeing a GP or therapist, I'm happy to work alongside them.

How many sessions?

Most people come for six to ten. We'll talk about what makes sense for you on the free call.
