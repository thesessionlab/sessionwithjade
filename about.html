About Jade

An unusual perspective on performance.

I am Jade Matthew, an actress, performance specialist, coach and the founder of Session.

My story

A life in performance, and a long interest in people.

For twenty years I have worked professionally as an actress across television, film, radio, audio drama and video games, with work for the BBC, HBO, Netflix, Channel 4, ITV and Sky and a BBC Audio Drama Award along the way. It has put me in a lot of rooms where communication, presence, preparation and the ability to perform under pressure matter enormously.

Alongside that career I built a specialist practice in psychology, confidence and human behaviour. I am a clinical hypnotherapist, a Master NLP Practitioner and Senior Audio Director at LAMDA, and I have coached more than 500 people, from individuals going through a difficult period to performers, artists and professionals working in demanding environments.

I have always been interested in the relationship between the person and the performance. We spend a lot of time on what other people see, how we speak and present ourselves, and much less on the nerves before the interview, the doubt that sits behind the success or the pressure of keeping up an image. My work brings those two sides together, so that you can show up fully without second guessing who you are.

The method

The Performance State Method.

How you feel affects how you communicate, your physical state affects your thinking, and the way you show up in the world feeds back into how you feel about yourself, so I work on all of it at once.

The method combines psychology, clinical hypnotherapy, NLP, breathwork, coaching and practical performance training. There is no universal formula: we work with what is actually happening for you and use the techniques that fit.
